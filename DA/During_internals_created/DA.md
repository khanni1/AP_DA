PROBABILITY CONCEPTS UNIT 1:
Independent vs Mutually Exclusive
This is another common viva trap.
Independent
One event does not affect the probability of the other.
Example:
Tossing a coin twice.
First toss doesn't affect second toss.
Mutually exclusive
Both events cannot occur together.
Example:
Getting Head and Tail on the same single coin toss.

conditional probability :
A∩B is the part of B where A also happens

Addition:

P(A ∪ B)
= P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)


Mutually exclusive:

P(A ∩ B) = 0

Therefore:

P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B)

Independent:

P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B)

Conditional:

P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)

Conditional multiplication:
P(A ∩ B) = P(A|B)P(B)
